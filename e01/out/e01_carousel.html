Why should you care?

These firms lend out other people's money. If you have a pension, a 401(k), a mutual fund or an insurance policy, some of your money may already sit with one of them.

Whoever manages this money decides who gets a loan, and at what price.

What I take from this

  1. LA is one giant plus a dense cluster. PIMCO is 71% of the total; the other 11 still add up to $1.50T.
  2. Two models, two talent markets. Bond houses scale with fewer people; private credit needs more hands per dollar.
  3. The Westside is the biggest cluster outside Newport. $751B, against $540B downtown.
Method. SEC registered adviser roster, Nov 2026. Firms with main offices in LA, Orange and Ventura counties whose business is mainly credit. Regulatory AUM is gross and as filed; filing dates differ by firm. Not investment advice.
